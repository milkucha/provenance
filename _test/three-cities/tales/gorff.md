## The tale

Gorff is workshop and archive territory — smiths, weavers, and the keepers of record who maintain the
city's own account of itself. Whatever the road brings in gets worked into something here, and
whatever happens gets written down, eventually, in Gorff's own hand. The city holds the transit
route's tollhouse on its own side of the road, and has for longer than anyone currently living can
personally remember.
