## The tale

By the old treaty settled after the Crossing War, Gorff keeps the tollhouse but owes Salthos
Cruzados a fixed share of what it collects, paid over each season. For the last three years running,
what actually arrives has come up short of what the tally says it should be. Nobody on the Salthos
side has called it theft outright, and nobody on Gorff's side has admitted to more than "the count's
been difficult lately" — whether it's an honest clerical slip somewhere in the counting, or coin
being quietly kept back, is not yet settled either way. What is certain, on both sides' own numbers,
is the gap itself and that it hasn't closed.
