## The tale

Salthos Cruzados sits exactly where the two roads out of Terfila and Gorff actually cross, and that's
the whole of what it is — it never became a market town or a workshop town in its own right, because
the crossing was always the point. The ruling house has governed it for three generations from a main
square built directly over the junction itself, and Prince Doran's court hears disputes that started
somewhere else and ended up here, because neither side trusts the other's ground to settle it on. A
temple stands at the square's far end, old enough that nobody agrees anymore on who built it first.
