## The tale

The market at Salthos Cruzados' main square has stood open to the sky for as long as anyone can
place it — stalls set up fresh each morning, packed away or ruined whenever the crossing's own
weather turns. The merchants who trade there, Gorff and Terfila hauliers passing through as much as
anyone actually local, have petitioned the Salthos house for years to put a roof over the square's
market row, so a season's goods stop getting lost to rain nobody could see coming until it was
already falling. Nothing has come of it yet — the timber and the coin both have to come from
somewhere, and the same treasury already carries the temple's own long-unfinished repairs. The two
projects compete for the same short shelf of resources, not for anyone's attention.
