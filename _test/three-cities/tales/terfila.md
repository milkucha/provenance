## The tale

Terfila is a port and market town, ships in and out on the tide, its wealth running through a handful
of merchant houses — the largest of which is Forliamus's, founded generations back on the trade that
moves along the transit route to Gorff. The market never really closes; the port just sets its rhythm
instead. What a Terfila household owns, it mostly owns because a Forliamus contract said so first.
