# Unknown — Open Questions and Gaps

This file collects gaps and open questions fed by three sources: `_lore/material/` (places named once
and never again, questions the source documents pose to themselves and leave blank, sections that are
headers with no content, identity questions no source resolves), `_lore/tales/` (a tale that raises a
question it doesn't itself answer), and `encodings.json`'s `hearsay` array (a character's claim that
points at a genuine gap in the objective record, rather than contradicting something already in it).
Cross-references to `encodings.json`'s `conflicts` array (disagreements *between* sources) are given as
`CONFLICT-##`; everything else here is a gap or an unposed/unanswered question rather than a
contradiction.
