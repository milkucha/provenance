# `_lore/characters/`

Master lore/character registry - one file per character under _lore/characters/, split off from the old combined _npcs/npcs/registry.json on 2026-08-05. This is what /enact and /character load and write; it has no Minecraft-facing fields at all, so lore development can proceed with no in-game representation. 'name' is canonical here (the Minecraft-side registry.json's display_name/taterzen_name are just a copy of it, made once a character is embodied). 'criterion' is what this character counts as a life well spent - derived once at creation from the collision of their knowledge sample with their backstory, then revisable only by a shock that references its anchor; its trusts/distrusts pair is the epistemology that criterion implies. 'life' holds 'lived' (how many scenes this character has had - their history, not secret) and 'deceased' (plain, non-secret boolean, false until /enact closes them out on their last scene, per scripts/lore/horizon.py's post-scene ending check). Their total span is NOT here: it lives in _lore/characters/lifespans.json, because this file is what /enact loads to PLAY a character, and a span sitting here would put the number in that character's own context at exactly the moment it must not be. Ask scripts/lore/horizon.py, which answers with a coarse band and never the figure. knowledge.education is frozen at creation, knowledge.experience appends freely, and criterion is sticky-but-revisable - it changes only when a referencing shock lands on a susceptible character.
