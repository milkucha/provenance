## The tale

Peregrins have been on pilgrimage for hundreds of years. Every year, they set out to encounter all
the temples in the world, in search of the diverse divinity. They are holy people, who once committed
to their own religion, took a bow of understanding and peace, promising to travel the land, the sea
and the air, in search of the other truths of the world. In doing this, they renounce their identity
and embrace the secret of their origin, for they are a canvas, upon which the cultures of the peoples
can find its expression, and permanence.
